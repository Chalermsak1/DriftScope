## Next conventional work

1. Expand a metadata-only frame toward additional genuinely qualifying components. Maintain target/raw/session/coordinate links before eligibility filtering. Do not split the large 128-proposal component or count additional tiles/repeats as independent groups. Check product-overlap coverage and authenticated observing/receiver records where available.
2. Preselect additional cadences within components and additional frequency tiles, with explicit inclusion probabilities. Estimate within-component cadence variation; seven placement-rich backgrounds cannot supply that variance or the main study's observing-environment precision.
3. Audit the remaining association/grouping losses using saved parent IDs, active support, path errors and fitted-corridor uncertainty. Separate localization/matching limitations from physical power loss; preserve this experiment before changing methods.
4. Design partial-time/intermittent searches and integration-edge improvements as separately versioned experiments. Account for additional search trials and threshold calibration. The present midpoint-quadrature diagnostics are observed checks, not exhaustive error bounds or a calibrated instrument simulator.
5. Add prospectively defined RFI/crowding and OFF-response tests. Zero native candidates in the current tiles is not a false-alarm calibration. Unknown OFF support must escalate rather than masquerade as evidence of absence.
6. Verify physical calibration, receiver/observing metadata, main-study precision and untouched held-out partitions before drawing population conclusions or beginning an LLM layer.
