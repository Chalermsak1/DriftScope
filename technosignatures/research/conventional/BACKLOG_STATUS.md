## Next executable milestones

1. **Receiver and cohort access:** validate the pointing/beam criterion from instrument references; record an amendment before further scientific selection. Expand metadata windows with all failure/exclusion counts retained. Acquire one detector-independent spectral tile across all six scans of the selected new C-band cadence under measured transfer/disk limits, checking every integration and source axis.
2. **Acquisition diversity:** meet the access-pilot target of 4–8 complete cadences over at least four actual independent components. Recompute linkage using actual session/raw/coordinate identities. Do not turn proposal count or reused cutouts into independent-session count.
3. **Sensitivity uncertainty:** add conditional-placement intervals under explicit randomization assumptions; use trial-level resampling for collision companions and preserve paired arms. Keep environmental intervals separate and unresolved when component support is inadequate.
4. **Native burden/comparator:** compare an established conventional implementation under a separately versioned score definition and matched support/workload. Measure aliases, event counts, forced-score thresholds, and policy losses before selecting a comparator.
5. **Morphology and scale:** extend integration convergence, OFF response, boundary and crowded tests across the full generated family matrix; profile before executing the 1,152-trial pilot. Retain all unsearchable/failed cases in the intended denominators.
6. **Freeze and evaluate:** only after independent data, physical validity, adequate precision and comparator selection, freeze the cohort/split/protocol and permit one locked technical comparison. LLM development remains gated by the approved plan.
